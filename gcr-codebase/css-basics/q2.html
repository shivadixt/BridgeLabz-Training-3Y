Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its royal palaces, forts, colorful markets, and rich heritage.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. It was one of India's earliest planned cities. The city is celebrated globally for its architectural symmetry, vast courtyards, and iconic terracotta pink walls.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal

The "Palace of Winds", famous for its honeycomb facade with 953 intricate jharokhas (small casements).

Amber Fort

A majestic hilltop fortress known for its artistic Hindu elements, grand ramparts, and Maota Lake reflections.

City Palace

A stunning royal complex blending Rajput and Mughal architecture with courtyards, gardens, and rich museums.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur is renowned for traditional Rajasthani folk music, Ghoomar dance, blue pottery, block printing, and world-famous cuisine like Dal Baati Churma and Ghewar.
